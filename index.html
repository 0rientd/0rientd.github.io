About Me

A developer who likes shipping things that actually get used

I've been building web systems for over five years, from the first conversation to the day they go live. Ruby on Rails is where I feel most at home, and I reach for Python or C++ when the problem asks for something else.

I've delivered sales and inventory platforms, custom APIs and Telegram bots for clients — usually as the person responsible for the whole thing: database, backend, deploy and the support that comes after.

Outside of client work I spend my time on open source and on lower-level things: graphics and games with C++ and SDL2, plus homebrew for the Nintendo 3DS. That is what keeps me curious about how things work under the hood.

Lately I have been running AI models locally with Ollama and LM Studio, and wiring them into agents and MCP tools — mostly to find out where they genuinely help in day-to-day development, and where they just get in the way.
